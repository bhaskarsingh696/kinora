
KINORA

Meaningful Connections. Shared Humanity.

KINORA is a platform designed to help people build genuine human connections through meaningful conversations, shared interests, and shared experiences.

We believe technology should bring people closer together—not leave them endlessly scrolling through content.

---

🌍 Our Vision

To create a world where meaningful human connection is accessible to everyone.

💡 What is KINORA?

KINORA is being designed as a social connection platform focused on real relationships rather than likes, followers, and endless feeds.

Our goal is to help people move beyond online interactions and develop meaningful, lasting connections.

✨ Core Features

The planned platform includes:

- Connection Pods: AI-organized groups of exactly six people who connect over a 14-day journey.
- AI-Powered Discovery: Find people through shared interests, goals, languages, and conversation preferences.
- Available Now: Discover people who are ready to connect.
- Find a Companion: Find someone to attend local events and casual outings with.
- New in Town: Connect with people in a new city.
- Shared Goals Partner: Find people working toward similar goals.
- Reconnect: Build continuity with meaningful connections.
- Conversation Rescue: Get help keeping conversations flowing.
- Shared Experiences Calendar: Discover and plan shared activities.
- Text-Based Community: Share thoughts, questions, ideas, and experiences.

🔐 Our Principles

- Genuine human connection over engagement metrics.
- Privacy and safety by design.
- Respect, consent, and inclusivity.
- No infinite-scroll content model.
- AI that supports human relationships rather than replacing them.
- Adults aged 18 and above.

🛠️ Development Status
